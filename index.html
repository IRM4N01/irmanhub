About Me

I come from a hospitality background, where I spent over seven years working in a fast-paced, customer-focused environment. While I valued the experience and skills I gained, I always felt the pull toward something more fulfilling — something that let me create, problem-solve, and build.

After starting a family, I decided to take a leap of faith and pursue that long-standing passion. With every project I build, I'm gaining technical skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and the confidence that I can create meaningful, lasting work in this field.

Outside of coding, I'm a passionate MMA and combat sports fan — there's something about the discipline and strategy of it that I find mirrors the problem-solving mindset of software engineering. I also unwind with anime and manga, always on the lookout for the next great series.
